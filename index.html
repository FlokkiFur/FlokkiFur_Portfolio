About Me

Hi! I'm Flokki, an artist based in Portugal who focuses primarily on 3D projects and drawings.

I began to deepen my artistic journey in 2020, when I took advantage of the quarantine to focus on my craft and share my drawings on social media. That motivation only grew stronger when I started college, where exposure to various forms of expression opened my eyes to new ways of creating, leading me to experiment and take my art to a new level.

Currently, I am pursuing a Bachelor's degree in [Proposital Emptiness] at [Proposital Emptiness] to deepen my understanding of digital art and better define my career path. Professionally, my goal is to work for a game company as a 3D modeler.

Outside of my portfolio, my biggest interests revolve around vídeo games (especially shooters and cartoon-styled projects); Animated media, ranging indie projects to full-length movies and series; And music (mainly Rock and Metal), where I also enjoy singing and drumming.

Languages: Native Portuguese, Fluent English
